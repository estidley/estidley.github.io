## PDF Anchor support

PDF Anchor is a local PDF reader for Android. Documents you open stay on the phone.

**Contact:** [estidley@gmail.com](mailto:estidley@gmail.com)

To remove a document, open the library and delete it. Uninstalling the app removes every copy, highlight, and note stored by PDF Anchor.
