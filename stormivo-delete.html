Delete your Stormivo data

In the Android app

Open About → Delete my data and account → Delete permanently. Keep an internet connection until deletion finishes. This stops monitoring and deletes the saved server position, preferences, alert history, notification association and anonymous Firebase account. If an error appears, reconnect and retry. Starting monitoring again creates a new account.

Request deletion without the app

Email estidley@gmail.com with subject “Stormivo data deletion”. Say that you want your Stormivo data deleted and whether you can still open the app. We will work with you to identify and verify the relevant installation before deleting records. Do not email passwords, authentication tokens or private keys. Anonymous accounts are not linked to your email, so an email address alone cannot identify the installation.

Server account deletion removes primary application records. Provider operational/security logs and any infrastructure backups are separate; contact us about a specific retention request. Uninstalling removes local app storage but does not request server deletion. Inactive saved positions are ordinarily cleared after 24 hours by the cleanup process.
