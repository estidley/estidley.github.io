Include your app version, Android version, approximate time of the issue and what happened. Never send Firebase tokens, passwords, service-account keys or exact location unless you deliberately choose to share it for troubleshooting.

Not receiving alerts?

  • Open Stormivo and start monitoring. Check that installation verification has finished.
  • Allow location and notifications, including both lightning notification channels.
  • Check the app's feed, connection and location-freshness messages.
  • Keep internet and location services enabled. Battery restrictions, force-stop, restarts and device settings may interrupt delivery.

This is not an emergency service. Do not rely on notification timing or an empty map for safety decisions.
