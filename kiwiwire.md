## KiwiWire

KiwiWire is an Android podcast player created and owned by Ethan Stidley.

Your library is stored on your device. There is no KiwiWire account or KiwiWire server. Discover uses an external podcast directory; playback and downloads connect to podcast hosts. Android backup and device transfer depend on your device settings. See the privacy policy for details.
